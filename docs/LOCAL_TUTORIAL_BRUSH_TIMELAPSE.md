# Local tutorial, brush and time-lapse revision

This revision is local and has not been published to GitHub.

## Tutorials

- The 19 steps use isolated captures of the actual raster editor and its menus, in both interface themes. Opening a tutorial in Gallery no longer captures Gallery as its demonstration.
- The preview focuses on the relevant controls. ColorDrop demonstrates moving the active color to an enclosed area, filling it, then adjusting threshold at a fixed fill location. Canvas navigation demonstrates pinch/rotation and zooming out into Canvas Atlas, panning, and activating an artwork.
- Mixing, Layers, group blending, sharing and recording lessons show their corresponding editor panels. The recording demonstration switches recording on and shows the duration and export controls.
- Demonstrations run in the preview without opening or changing the user's artwork. Reduced-motion settings show a static demonstration state.

The source captures are generated by `TutorialSceneCaptureTest` into `artifacts/tutorial-scenes`. The packaged subset lives under `android/app/src/main/assets/tutorials`. Regenerate these when the corresponding editor controls change. `TutorialExportExperienceTest` renders every step for visual review and verifies that its demonstration changes over time.

## Brushes

- Painting now includes distinct gouache, dry-bristle and wet-bristle behavior, with stroke-aligned bristle marks and canvas-anchored grain.
- Materials: Dry Surface, Linen Weave and Leather Grain.
- Organic: Soft Grain, Moss Stipple and Leaf Scatter.
- Vintage: Faded Ink, Etching and Halftone Print.
- New tips use separate native texture patterns on CPU and GPU. Eraser variants, undo/redo and custom brush copies retain the selected tip.
- The new painting and material preview strokes are rendered by the native engine and packaged as transparent swatches.

These are procedural brush textures; they are not a water-fluid simulation. Their feel and performance still need physical-tablet testing.

## Time-lapse

- Video controls offer 30 seconds, 1 minute or 2 minutes. The full saved recording is fitted to that duration, with short opening and closing holds.
- Visible recording controls no longer expose saved-frame counts or frame-rate details.
- Export displays preparation, encoding percentage, file-selection, saving and saved/error/cancelled states. Saved is shown only after the chosen output stream has been written and closed.
- Duplicate export taps reopen the current progress card instead of starting another job.
- Recording append bookkeeping caches file count, next index and storage use instead of scanning the recording directory after every capture.
- Output is H.264 MP4 at 24 fps. Playback smoothness still depends on the amount of recorded drawing data; short recordings repeat source images to meet the chosen duration.
