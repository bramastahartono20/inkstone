# Inkstone Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 November 2026

Inkstone is a drawing app for Android made by Vincentius Bramasta Hartono. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

## Summary

Inkstone does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking. It does not request the Android internet permission, so it cannot send anything over the network.

## Your artwork and settings

Your artwork, brushes, palettes and preferences are saved in the app's private storage on your device. They are not uploaded anywhere. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them, so export a backup first if you want to keep them.

## Files you choose

When you import an image or export artwork, Android's file picker opens. Inkstone reads only the file you pick and writes only to the location you choose. What happens to an exported file after that, for example sharing it, is up to you and the app you share it with.

## Permissions

On Android 8 and 9, Inkstone asks for storage permission only when you save an image to the Photo Gallery, and uses it only for that.

## Children

Inkstone is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
